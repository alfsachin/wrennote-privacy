Wrennote privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026

In short

Wrennote works entirely on your phone. It does not collect, send or share any of your information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

No internet

Wrennote contains no code to go online. On Android it also has no permission to use the internet. Speech recognition, reading aloud and reading text from photos are done by AI models that run on your phone.

Microphone

When you tap the mic, Wrennote listens so it can turn your speech into text. The audio is kept in the phone's memory only while it is being transcribed, is never saved to storage, and is erased after use.

Camera

When you take a photo to read text, the photo is kept in memory only while the text is read, is never saved to storage or your photo library, and is released right after.

Your notes

Notes you keep are stored in Wrennote's private storage on your phone until you delete them. You can turn off keeping notes in Voice settings. Wrennote opts out of cloud backups, so your notes are not copied to any cloud service by the app. Uninstalling Wrennote deletes all of its data.

If you use Back up, Wrennote saves a file of your notes to a folder you pick. If that folder is synced to a cloud service, that service's own privacy policy applies to the file.

Settings

Your chosen voice, speed, theme, font and text size are saved on your phone only.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through your app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store). The store handles payment under its own privacy policy. Wrennote only learns whether the unlock has been bought, and keeps that on your phone.

Children

Wrennote is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
