Wrennote

The notebook you can talk to and listen to.

Dictate notes, hear them read aloud in a natural voice, and scan text with the camera. All on your phone. Nothing is ever sent online.

Talk instead of typing

Speak for a sentence or for minutes. Punctuation and capitals appear by themselves, and filler words like “um” are tidied away without changing what you said.

▶

Listen to any note

A natural voice reads your notes back. Pick a voice, change the speed, tap a sentence to jump there. Prices, links and numbers read the way a person says them.

📷

Scan text from a page

Frame the paragraph you want, pinch to zoom, and the words drop into your note, ready to edit or hear.

Private because it can't go online.

  • Speech recognition, voices and photo reading run on your phone, even in airplane mode.
  • Recordings and photos are never saved. Only the notes you keep are stored, on your phone.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no cloud backup of your notes.
  • Back up your notes to a file whenever you like, free.
