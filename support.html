Wrennote support

Help with Wrennote on iPhone and Android

Contact

Email wrennote.app@gmail.com. You can also tap the shield button in the app, then Send feedback, which fills in your app version and phone model.

Does Wrennote need the internet?

No. Dictation, reading aloud and reading text from photos all run on your phone, even in airplane mode. Wrennote never sends anything anywhere.

Dictation tips

Speak at a normal pace, close to the phone. The text you see while talking is a quick draft. When you stop, Wrennote reads the whole recording again for the best accuracy and removes filler words like "um". It only ever removes words, never adds or changes them.

Scanning text with the camera

Drag the corners of the frame around the text you want and pinch to zoom. Hold the phone flat over the page and steady while you tap. In dim light, turn on the light button.

The free trial and unlock

Everything is free for 7 days. After that, a single $2.99 purchase unlocks Wrennote for good. There is no subscription. Your notes stay on your phone either way.

Restoring your purchase

On a new phone, sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. On iPhone, tap Restore next to the Unlock button. On Android it restores by itself.

Moving or backing up notes

Open Notes and tap Back up to save all notes to a file in a folder you choose. On another phone, tap Restore and pick that file. Wrennote does not use cloud backup, so this is the way to keep a copy.
